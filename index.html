Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026 · Last updated 4 October 2026 · Applies to the Forage extension for Google Chrome

The short version. Forage runs entirely inside your own browser. It has no servers, no user accounts and no analytics. It never sends your spreadsheets, your order information or anything else to its developer or to anyone else. The only websites it contacts are the two vendor portals it reads — Four Hands and Leftbank Art — using the sign-in you already have there.

What Forage is

Forage is a Chrome extension for interior designers. You give it your project spreadsheet (an FF&E or procurement schedule); it checks the vendor portals you are already signed in to, and shows which items have shipped, been delayed or been cancelled, compared with what your spreadsheet says. It can also produce an updated copy of your spreadsheet for you to download.

What Forage reads

  • The spreadsheet you choose. When you pick a file, Forage reads it inside your browser. The file is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your orders at Four Hands and Leftbank Art. Forage asks fourhands.com and www.leftbankart.com for your account's order information — order numbers, product codes, item descriptions, quantities, order status, and ship and arrival dates. It does this with the sign-in you have already established in Chrome; Forage never sees or stores your password. To look up particular orders, it sends those vendors order numbers taken from your spreadsheet — numbers the vendor issued to you in the first place.
  • What it ignores. Four Hands' order report also contains the delivery address and phone number for each order. Forage discards those fields as it reads the report; they are never displayed, saved or included in any download.

Forage does not read any other website, your browsing history, your bookmarks, other tabs, or your email.

What Forage saves, and where

So that Forage can list your projects and tell you what changed since the last check, it keeps a short record of each project:

  • The project itself: the name you gave it, when you created it and last checked it, how many items it had, how many needed your attention, and how many were at each stage (ordered, shipped and so on).
  • Its most recent check: for each item its name, room, vendor, order number and product code, the status and estimated arrival date the vendor reported, and when it was checked. Forage also uses this to recognise which project a spreadsheet belongs to when you load it.

So that it only has to ask once how to read your spreadsheets, Forage also keeps your setup, once you confirm it: the column names of each spreadsheet layout and which column holds what; what your own status words mean; which vendor each vendor name on your sheets refers to; and whether your spreadsheet goes back into a board or app. Setup holds no item, order or client details. Reset setup in the panel's settings deletes it; Clear saved history does not.

  • All of this is stored in Chrome's local extension storage, on your computer only. It is not synced to your Google account and is not sent anywhere.
  • Only the latest check of each project is kept. Beyond 100 projects, the one checked longest ago is deleted automatically.
  • You can delete your projects and their checks at any time with Clear saved history, and your setup with Reset setup, both in the Forage panel's settings. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all of it.

Forage stores nothing else. Your spreadsheet itself is not kept once the panel is closed.

Downloads

When you choose Download updated sheet, Forage creates a spreadsheet in your browser and hands it to Chrome to save where you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

Sample data

The Try sample data button runs Forage on an invented project with invented orders. It contacts no website and saves nothing.

What Forage never does

  • It does not send any data to its developer. There is no Forage server.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, for any purpose.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not store or have access to your passwords.
  • It does not load or run code from the internet; everything it runs, including the fonts its panel uses, is included in the extension as reviewed by the Chrome Web Store.
  • It does not use your data for anything other than showing you the status of your own orders.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

Forage's use of the information described here complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide Forage's single purpose — showing interior designers the current status of their orders — and is not transferred to anyone.

Children

Forage is a business tool for design professionals. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle information about children.

Changes to this policy

If Forage ever changes what it reads, saves or sends — for example, if a future version checks a licence key online — this policy will be updated to say exactly what is involved before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
